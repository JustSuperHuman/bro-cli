Palette data in t3-default-light.json and t3-default-dark.json is extracted
without color changes from T3_CHAT_THEME.colors and T3_CHAT_THEME.variants.dark:
https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code/blob/5a20d59db38abef8e0b98cfe47fed81d074bcecc/packages/shared/src/themePalettes.ts#L238-L371

These are the defaults used for omitted native T3 theme roles. They are not the
separate T3_CODE_LIGHT_THEME_COLORS / T3_CODE_DARK_THEME_COLORS palettes.
