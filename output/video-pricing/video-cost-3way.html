How the numbers are built

Higgsfield: live credit quote ÷ clip length × plan price ÷ 1,200 credits ÷ share of credits you actually use. Using 75% of your credits makes every second 1.33× dearer; 50% makes it 2×. Unlimited windows excluded.

OpenRouter: published per-second (or per-token, converted at 720p/24 fps) rates plus the 5.5% credit-purchase fee.

OpenLux: official upstream price × the route's group multiplier (OpenLux's own rule: at multiplier 0.65, $1 of official price costs $0.65). $1 of OpenLux credit costs $1. Wan 3.0 and both Veo rows come straight from OpenLux's catalogue; the other rows apply the multiplier to the official list rate (as listed on OpenRouter).
